01 / Executive summary

The uncomfortable conclusion

Do not build a generic fraud scorer, a chargeback writer, a reconciliation chatbot, a subscription retry bot, or a conversational checkout. Razorpay has already shipped or publicly demonstrated most of those directions, and public repositories show that many applicants reached the same answer.

Best opportunity

RupeeFence

Agent transaction integrity: user intent becomes a verifiable mandate; every MCP/API action is checked for scope, freshness, merchant binding, replay, and approval.

Why this wins

It is one level deeper

It improves the safety and interoperability of the agentic payment rail Razorpay is actively launching, rather than cloning an agent that operates inside it.

Proof in 30 seconds

Attack, block, prove

A stale cart, substituted merchant, replayed tool call, or over-limit refund is denied before Razorpay sees it. A valid test-mode order passes and is audited.

Recommendation: build a narrow, defense-only control plane with one real Razorpay test-mode path and a large deterministic adversarial harness. The AI extracts intent and explains decisions; it never owns money authority.

What Razorpay is screening for

  • Problem taste, not buzzword selection
  • Working code and trustworthy structure
  • AI judgment: model where useful, deterministic logic where safer
  • Failure recovery and honest limitations
  • Evidence a builder can ship systems, not just prompts

Confidence and caveats

  • Rules and product launches below are confirmed from official Razorpay pages unless labelled otherwise.
  • Participant counts are a public GitHub search sample, not an official census.
  • AI product pages make company claims; do not repeat them as independent benchmarks.
  • There is no published cutoff time, team-size rule, deployment requirement, or all-purpose disqualification list.

05 / Track deep dives

Choose the layer below the prompt

Each track below translates the literal brief into engineering work, then distinguishes product value from demo theatre. The 40 ideas are intentionally compact but each has a user, business case, AI boundary, integration/data plan, evaluation, safety, wow moment, competition and score.

Track 1 / Agentic commerce

Literal: grow merchant revenue or make a merchant transactable by an AI buyer, using Razorpay test-mode APIs/end-to-end checkout. Underlying: identity, consent, transaction integrity, agent reputation, merchant semantics, bounded authority. Panel: will notice a real protocol/security primitive; will dismiss a chatbot storefront.

White space: authorization and evidence between user intent, agent, merchant, cart and payment. AP2's signed mandates and ACP checkout objects validate that this is an emerging infrastructure problem, not sci-fi.

Track 2 / Risk manager

Literal: detector, verifier or auto-responder for one loss class, with precision/recall on held-out data; defense-only. Underlying: asymmetric false-positive cost, drift, investigation throughput, explainability and merchant trust. Panel: values labels, leakage control, calibrated decisions and safe review.

White space: risk operations and drift intervention, not another transaction classifier. Existing Vulcan/Shield/Thirdwatch and graph projects make raw AUC hard to differentiate.

Track 3 / Revenue recovery

Literal: detect risk, diagnose cause, choose and execute bounded recovery; prove measured money recovered, compliance stopping rules and audit. Underlying: incremental lift after contact cost, timing, channel fatigue, root cause and experiment design. Panel: wants a causal baseline, not “revenue at risk” charts.

White space: merchant-specific experimentation and recovery policy learning. But public activity and Agent Studio make it the most crowded track.

Track 4 / Finance controller

Literal: close one finance-ops loop over 50+ synthetic records; match rate plus exceptions. Underlying: high-throughput deterministic matching, abstention, evidence, owner routing and accounting correctness. Panel: wants the exception list, not a cherry-picked match.

White space: fee/settlement causality and proof trees. Generic reconciliation is already Razorpay Recon and a large GitHub cluster.

Track 5 / Open

Literal: real problem, meaningful AI, working product, evidence of value; same execution/reliability/depth bar. Underlying: problem taste outside the obvious funnel. Panel: asks why this belongs to Razorpay and why a dedicated track was not enough.

Evidence: Fix My Itch lists high-scoring pains such as SME payment terms (82.8), accountable pickup (79), supplier verification (76), and micro-SME invoice work (67.5). These are real signals, but open-track integration/data access is weaker.

Protocol reality check

Google AP2 (v0.2 repo, created 30 May 2025) defines agent-performed payment security and human-present/not-present flows around mandates and verifiable credentials. OpenAI/Stripe ACP is a beta open standard with checkout session APIs. Coinbase x402 makes a paid resource available through HTTP 402 and signed payment headers.

These protocols leave room for a Razorpay-native adapter that checks permission and state, rather than another protocol explainer.

Track 1: AI Growth & Agentic Commerce

1. RupeeFence / signed mandate firewall D 7F 8N 9W 10

Concept/user: Merchant and payment platform teams get a policy firewall that converts user intent into a short-lived signed mandate and validates the exact cart, merchant, amount, currency, expiry, approval and state version before any Razorpay MCP/API action. Why it matters: one wrong or replayed autonomous payment destroys trust. AI: LLM extracts intent and explains; deterministic schema, cryptographic binding, policy and state checks decide. Not LLM alone: signatures, replay, ceilings and idempotency must fail closed.

Integration/data/eval: official Razorpay MCP or REST test-mode order/payment-link tools; webhooks payment.captured/payment.failed/order.paid; synthetic attack traces plus real test-mode calls. Compare raw agent vs firewall over 10,000 traces: unsafe-call rate, benign pass rate, false denials, p95 latency, replay detection, audit completeness. Safety/demo: valid order passes; stale cart, merchant substitution, over-limit refund and duplicate call are denied before forwarding. Closest: SENTINEL, OrderGuard, AgentPay Nexus. Moat: mandate-to-cart-to-payment proof and conformance harness. B 10T 10M 10R 10K 6

06 / Saturation and white space

Where not to get lost

Buildathon competitor map

LOW protocol conformance plus signed mandate/cart/state proof; post-purchase agent receipt integrity; beneficiary-change governance; payment-degradation circuit breaker.

MEDIUM agent-readable catalog; fraud drift; authorized-scam detection; settlement fee-leak analysis; evidence provenance.

HIGH generic conversational checkout; upsell/cross-sell agent; generic fraud classifier; chargeback auto-responder; failed-payment retry/dunning; subscription recovery; generic reconciliation; cashflow dashboard.

White-space map

Already solved by Razorpay: basic payment links, standard subscriptions/retries, Smart Collect reconciliation, core RTO intelligence, dispute workflow, Agent Studio exemplars.

Crowded with startups: chargeback automation, smart retries, receivables voice agents, fraud scoring, bank reconciliation.

Technically interesting but weak business value: multi-agent negotiation for its own sake; protocol visualizers without execution proof; generic AI CFO chat.

Valuable but hard to prove: live fraud lift, live causal recovery, cross-merchant reputation without network data, real regulatory approvals.

Valuable + feasible + relatively unexplored: a model-agnostic transaction-integrity layer that plugs into real Razorpay MCP/test mode and proves safe/unsafe calls at scale.

One level deeper: most entrants ask “what should the agent do?” The better question is “what must be true before an agent is allowed to do it, and what evidence survives after it does?” That is the RupeeFence thesis.

  • Avoids feature cloning by solving authorization, integrity and evidence.

10 / The project to build

RupeeFence

Track: AI Growth & Agentic Commerce

Project: RupeeFence, a signed mandate and transaction-integrity firewall for AI agents using Razorpay.

User: payment platform engineers and merchants exposing payment tools to buyer or operations agents.

Problem: a capable agent can call the right API with the wrong amount, merchant, cart state, timing, scope or replayed authorization.

One-line pitch: “RupeeFence turns natural-language intent into a short-lived, verifiable spending mandate, then proves the exact state an AI agent is allowed to send to Razorpay.”

Why now / why Razorpay cares

Razorpay is actively shipping Agentic Payments, UPI Reserve Pay, MCP, Agent Studio, a CLI and ChatGPT/Claude surfaces. The company needs agents that are useful without making the payment rail untrustworthy. RupeeFence is a merchant-controlled safety plane that could sit around those surfaces.

Why judges remember it: the live failure is not an LLM hallucination on a slide; it is a real tool call denied before execution, with a signed reason and tamper-evident audit.

Why competitors are unlikely to match the exact build

Some participants have a generic policy proxy or an allow/deny layer. Fewer will bind a canonical user intent to a cart snapshot, merchant identity, amount, expiry, approval and webhook outcome, then test it with protocol mutation and replay at scale. That combination is the differentiator, not the word “agent”.

11 / Product specification

Build the smallest credible product

User journey

  1. Merchant defines a policy: allowed merchant IDs/categories, max single spend, daily budget, payment methods, expiry, approval rules and refund/payout restrictions.
  2. Buyer or operator states intent: “Buy this cart from this merchant for up to Rs 2,000 before 6 PM.”
  3. Intent Parser returns strict JSON. It may ask a clarifying question; it cannot call money tools.
  4. Mandate Builder signs the canonical intent and records nonce, scope, expiry, user/agent key, merchant binding and policy version.
  5. Agent proposes a cart. Cart Resolver snapshots item IDs, quantities, tax, shipping, amount, currency, merchant ID and catalog version.
  6. RupeeFence checks signature, scope, freshness, cart hash, amount, merchant, nonce, action type, approval and idempotency.
  7. Valid low-risk order/payment-link action is forwarded to Razorpay test mode; sensitive/refund/payout action pauses for approval.
  8. Webhook Ingestor verifies the HMAC, deduplicates and records payment outcome. The audit receipt shows intent -> cart -> tool call -> Razorpay ID -> outcome.

Agent architecture and boundaries

Model goals

Parse ambiguous intent; identify missing constraints; classify a tool request; explain a deterministic decision in plain language. Context is the current user intent, signed mandate, cart snapshot and policy facts. Memory is only the merchant policy and event ledger.

Tools: read catalog, quote cart, propose action, request approval, read payment status. The agent never receives a Razorpay secret and never bypasses the gateway.

Hard boundaries

Permissions are scoped per action. Refunds, payouts, beneficiary changes and any over-budget action require approval. All calls carry nonce and idempotency key. Stop on stale state, signature failure, unavailable audit append, ambiguous identity, unknown tool schema or low confidence.

Failure modes: deny and explain; never “try another tool” automatically after a policy failure.

12 / Integration and proof

Razorpay integration plan

Real test-mode surface

  • Use official Razorpay MCP as the primary demonstration adapter when credentials are available; discover and record the tool manifest at startup rather than hard-coding an unverified list.
  • Fallback REST adapter: create an order through POST /v1/orders; create a standard Payment Link through the documented Payment Links API; fetch payments; refund only through a deliberately denied test-mode path.
  • Use test keys only. Test cards expose a mock success/failure page; success@razorpay and failure@razorpay are documented UPI test IDs. Standard Payment Links are limited to 30 per business in test mode; UPI Payment Links are not supported in test mode.

Events and invariants

  • Ingest and verify payment.failed, payment.captured, order.paid, refund events and, if used, subscription/settlement events.
  • Razorpay documents that event order is not guaranteed and a failed webhook can be followed by captured. Reducer must be idempotent and order-tolerant.
  • For settlement evidence, the documented recon endpoint is /v1/settlements/recon/combined?year=yyyy&month=mm; use read-only data only.
  • Never include live keys, card numbers, CVVs or personal data in the repo/video.

Repeatable evaluation harness

Headline result format: “Across 10,000 held-out tool traces, RupeeFence reduced unsafe forwards from baseline A to B, passed C% of valid actions, detected D% of replays, and added P95 latency of E ms. It made zero financial decisions with an LLM alone.”

Honesty ruleLabel synthetic, replayed and real test-mode rows separately. Do not claim recovered money or fraud prevention from a simulator.
